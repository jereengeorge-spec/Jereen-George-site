    Calibrated from your own apps (1:46 AM reading): the car drew 47 A at 10.8 kW into the battery while the panel metered 11.19 kW — so the charger runs about 96.5% efficient, and cost is figured on the panel draw (what the meter bills) while charge time is figured on what reaches the battery. Rated range is 237 mi at 100%, from the observed 211 mi at 89%; driving consumption is 33 kWh / 100 mi, which is why a full battery covers about 227 real miles rather than the rated 237. Also assumed: 30 nights per month, 20% DC fast-charging overhead (session losses, taper and idle fees), and energy outside the window billed at 20¢/kWh. The broken-loop strategy replenishes only the first 100 miles at home and buys the rest mid-shift at the DCFC rate. The charge-session figures (amperage table, nightly charge time) follow the plug-in readout — set it to what the dash actually shows on a lighter night; the monthly cost and savings tiles stay on the typical-day mileage.
